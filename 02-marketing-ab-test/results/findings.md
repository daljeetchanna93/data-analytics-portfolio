# Results — Marketing Campaign A/B Test

## Primary test
| Group | Users | Conversions | Conversion rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Control (PSA) | 23,524 | 420 | 1.79% |
| Test (Ad) | 564,577 | 14,423 | 2.55% |

- Absolute lift: **+0.77 percentage points** (95% CI +0.60 to +0.94)
- Relative lift: **+43.1%** (bootstrap 95% CI +30.5% to +57.4%)
- z = 7.37, p = 1.7 × 10⁻¹³ (chi-square p identical) → **reject H₀**

## Business impact
- Incremental conversions caused by ads: **4,343** (95% CI 3,360 – 5,326)
- Conversions the ad group would have had anyway: ~10,080 (70%)

| 101–200 | 10.64% | 17.68% | +66% | Yes |
| 200+ | 15.50% | 15.58% | — | No |

## Timing (ad group)
- Best days: Monday 3.32%, Tuesday 3.04%; weakest: Saturday 2.13%, Thursday 2.16%
- 2–9 PM peak-hour users: 2.90% vs 1–4 AM: 1.07%

## Recommendations
1. Keep the campaign; report ROI on incremental conversions.
2. Cap frequency near 200 impressions and reinvest in users under 50.
3. Weight budget toward Mon/Tue and 2–9 PM.
4. Run a randomized frequency-cap test next.
