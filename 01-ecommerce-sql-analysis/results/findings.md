## Recommendations
1. Carrier SLAs plus proactive delay messaging, starting with RJ, BA and ES.
2. A second-purchase campaign to lift the 3% repeat rate.
3. A key-account program for the top 297 sellers.
4. Installment-based promotions for high-ticket categories.
